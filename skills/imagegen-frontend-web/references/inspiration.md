# Optional inspiration

Starting points only; adapt them to the brief and the brand. Do not use all of them.

## Components for one site (pick about four)
Gapless bento grid; staggered square masonry; layered card deck; hover-accordion slices (imply expansion through proportions); curated polaroid arc; product UI panel stack with real flows; oversized metrics strip (only with real data); split testimonial wall; layered image crops; brand marquee (only with legible logos).

## Theme and type pairings
- Pristine light: cream or paper with dark ink; editorial serif plus clean sans.
- Deep dark: graphite with one restrained accent; refined grotesk.
- Bold studio solid: one controlled field color (forest, oxblood, royal blue) with crisp UI; expressive display type.
- Quiet premium neutral: sand, stone, taupe; Swiss-style hierarchy.

## Narrative spines
Artifact or collectible; journey with waypoints; precision instrument; living system; stage and spotlight; archive or dossier.

## Second-read motifs (use one, once)
A controlled asymmetric bleed; one oversized numeral or punctuation mark that structures the layout; a single material switch (paper to metal); a narrow vertical editorial side-rail; a macro crop carrying the brand color.

## Brief mapping
- Minimalist or Swiss: mini hero, solid surfaces, stacked center, generous negative space.
- Editorial or fashion: mid or giant hero, graded or duotone photography, off-grid offsets.
- Cinematic or luxury: giant hero, full-bleed image with tonal overlay, bottom-left text.
- SaaS or fintech: mid hero, clear product framing, subtle palette-matched gradients.
- E-commerce: product-led composition and unmistakable CTAs.
